# Mission 001 — First Evidence-Backed Dollar

Status: SANDBOX DESIGN

Objective: discover and validate one low-upfront-cost problem that can be solved with an existing or rapidly buildable digital/service capability, then prepare a human-approved pilot offer.

Constraints:
- upfront experiment budget <= $25 before explicit override;
- no paid advertising in discovery;
- no unsolicited message is sent by the system;
- no fabricated testimonials, demand, customers or revenue;
- prefer time-to-first-cash <= 14 days;
- prefer fulfillment that can initially be performed manually or semi-automatically;
- no securities/derivatives/crypto trading as the revenue experiment;
- no production launch before audit + human approval.

Success:
1. at least three independent external evidence points for the problem;
2. a named customer segment;
3. a falsifiable willingness-to-pay test;
4. a sandbox deliverable or demo;
5. transparent pilot price/cost assumptions;
6. human-approved pilot;
7. first settled external customer payment recorded by MINT.

Stop:
- evidence contradicts the problem;
- acquisition economics exceed experiment ceiling;
- legal/compliance issue cannot be resolved;
- fulfillment cannot meet the promised outcome;
- customer harm or deceptive framing is required to make the offer attractive.
