# AI Company North Star

Build a portfolio of useful, lawful, measurable economic systems that can discover problems, research them, create bounded solutions, package approved offers, serve customers, measure settled economics and improve from evidence.

## Optimization hierarchy
1. Truth and safety constraints.
2. Useful customer outcome.
3. Settled contribution after direct/compute/human cost.
4. Repeatability and retention.
5. Speed and automation.

Never optimize vanity activity, fabricated demand, expected revenue presented as cash, or autonomy for its own sake.

## Graduation ladder
SIMULATION -> SANDBOX -> HUMAN-APPROVED PILOT -> LIMITED PRODUCTION -> MEASURED SCALE

An agent/workflow advances only with evidence. FORGE compares variants; AUDITOR verifies claims; GOVERNOR owns consequential approvals; COST CONTROLLER enforces budgets.
