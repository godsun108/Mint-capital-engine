## Publishing guardrails

1. Publish only material that is accurate, tested where executable, and meaningfully helpful without purchase.
2. Link naturally to a matching offer with clear price and what is delivered; avoid keyword stuffing and fake urgency.
3. Keep every page indexable only if it offers substantial unique information; do not mass-produce thin doorway pages.
4. Use a distinct lowercase `src` code from the table for each campaign link and validate that it is recorded in acquisition aggregates.
5. Verify real demand through Google Search Console or other first-party search data before claiming impressions, rankings, or prioritizing by volume.
6. Do not automatically publish externally or spend on advertising without separate approval.
7. Review weekly: indexed pages, impressions, clicks, visits (nonunique), checkout starts, paid purchases, and verified fulfillment. Keep these metrics distinct.
