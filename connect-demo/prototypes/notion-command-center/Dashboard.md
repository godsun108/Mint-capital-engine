# Personal Command Center

**North Star:** Define one measurable outcome for the next 90 days.

## Today
- Choose one meaningful action from Actions.
- Check upcoming deadlines and blockers.
- Complete physical-life basics as well as project work.

## Weekly review
1. Review active Projects and update their Next Milestone.
2. Move blocked actions to Waiting; record what unlocks them.
3. Archive completed tasks and pick no more than three weekly priorities.
4. Capture lessons and decide what to stop doing.

## Views to configure in Notion
- Actions / Today: Due is today and Status is not Done.
- Actions / This Week: Due is within next 7 days and Status is not Done.
- Projects / Active: Status is Active.
- Projects / Waiting: Status is Blocked.

This is an editable template, not an AI agent or automated Notion workspace.
