## Supplier-wide requirements verified
Printful recommends sRGB IEC61966-2.1 and generally 150–300 DPI depending on product. PNG is useful for transparency; the product-specific guideline takes precedence. File library max 200 MB / 20,000 × 20,000 px. Design Maker can show print-quality warnings and generate mockups. **None of these generic requirements establish a specific W101 print rectangle or AOP bleed size.**
