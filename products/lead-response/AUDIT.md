# Launch Readiness Audit — Mission 001

## PASS
- Product solves a concrete measurable workflow problem.
- Sandbox can operate without lead PII.
- No autonomous customer communications.
- Revenue claims are not embedded in the diagnostic.
- Opportunity estimate requires customer-supplied assumptions and is labeled not revenue.
- Fulfillment can begin manually.
- Experiment has a small explicit budget ceiling.
- MINT settlement truth remains authoritative.

## OPEN EVIDENCE
- Willingness to pay for this specific diagnostic has not been demonstrated.
- No pilot customer has used the product.
- No measured customer outcome exists.
- No validated acquisition channel exists.
- Initial vertical is not yet selected.

## HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED BEFORE PILOT
- approve public/customer-facing offer wording;
- approve chosen price variant;
- approve any outbound prospect communication;
- approve payment-provider activation/use if needed;
- approve any production deployment.

## BLOCKERS TO AUTOMATED RECOVERY
Before sending email/SMS/calls on behalf of a business, separately design consent, opt-out, sender identity, provider, retention, security, rate-limit and human-handoff controls. This pilot intentionally stops before that boundary.

## Auditor verdict
READY FOR HUMAN-APPROVED DESIGN-PARTNER / PAID-PILOT VALIDATION.

NOT READY for autonomous outreach, autonomous customer communication, revenue claims, or scaled production.
