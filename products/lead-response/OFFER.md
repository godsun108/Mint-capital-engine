# Lead Response Diagnostic — Pilot Offer

Status: DRAFT / NOT PUBLIC

## Promise
See how consistently your business responds to inbound leads, where response gaps occur, and what workflow changes are worth testing.

## Pilot deliverable
- privacy-minimal lead-response analysis
- response-window scorecard
- unanswered-lead count
- business-hours vs after-hours comparison
- prioritized workflow-gap report
- optional assumption-based opportunity model using customer-provided inputs
- 30-minute findings review
- recovery-workflow recommendation

## Not included
- autonomous customer messaging
- CRM replacement
- guaranteed revenue/conversion improvement
- fabricated attribution of unanswered leads to lost sales
- legal/compliance advice

## Pricing experiment
Test willingness to pay rather than assuming it.

Variant A: $49 diagnostic pilot.
Variant B: $99 diagnostic + implementation plan.
Variant C: $0 diagnostic for a tightly limited design partner in exchange for structured product feedback — never a fabricated testimonial.

No variant is published or offered until human approval.

## Fulfillment target
Customer supplies a privacy-minimal export or manually prepared timestamps. Generate report, manually verify anomalies, review findings, recommend the smallest next workflow change. Target fulfillment: <= 60 minutes of operator time for the first pilots.

## Refund/quality principle
If the supplied data cannot support a useful diagnostic, say so before presenting conclusions.
