# Pilot Fulfillment Runbook

1. Confirm the business understands the sandbox scope.
2. Request timestamps/channel/outcome only; discourage PII.
3. Validate input rows and timezone assumptions.
4. Run diagnostic.
5. Manually inspect outliers and invalid timestamps.
6. Generate scorecard and gap list.
7. If business supplied average job value/close rate, label any dollar output assumption-based and not recoverable revenue.
8. Conduct findings review.
9. Ask structured willingness-to-pay/product-value questions.
10. Record only settled payment as revenue in MINT.
11. Record time and direct costs.
12. Auditor reviews pilot outcome before automation scope expands.

Never contact the business's leads during this pilot.
