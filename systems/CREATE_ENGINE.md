# CREATE ENGINE v0.1

Purpose: produce legitimate earned USD through things MINT can help create, sell and fulfill.

Lanes:
- software/digital product
- productized service
- subscription
- licensing
- commission pipeline
- marketplace/resale after verified economics

Automation target:
DEMAND SIGNAL → OFFER → COST/PRICE → ASSET/DELIVERABLE → PAYMENT RAIL → PUBLISH/OUTREACH → FULFILL → FOLLOW UP → PAYMENT → SETTLE

MINT should favor offers with low upfront cash, short time-to-cash, repeatable fulfillment and high automation potential.

A listing, lead, click, invoice or payment intent is not revenue. Revenue enters the settled ledger only after confirmed payment settlement.
