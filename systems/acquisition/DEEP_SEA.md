# DEEP SEA — MINT Leveraged Distribution Doctrine

## Mission
Find distribution environments where one strong product can reach materially more qualified buyers than an isolated owned page.

Deep Sea is not mass posting. It seeks **distribution leverage**.

## Oceans

### MARKETPLACE DEMAND
Digital/physical marketplaces with existing buyer intent.

### ECOSYSTEM DISTRIBUTION
App/plugin/integration marketplaces where a product becomes part of another platform's workflow.

### LAUNCH & DISCOVERY
Product launch communities, directories and catalogs with genuine product-discovery behavior.

### PARTNERSHIP DISTRIBUTION
Affiliates, referrals, creators, newsletters, agencies, resellers, bundles and complementary-product partnerships. Outreach requires applicable authorization and platform/rule compliance.

### LICENSING & B2B
Reusable assets, software, templates, data products and systems sold/licensed to businesses or distributors.

### EMBEDDED COMMERCE
APIs, widgets, integrations and white-label components that create repeated distribution through other products.

### PHYSICAL NETWORKS
POD, supplier-fulfilled and later stocked-product marketplaces after supplier/economics/returns/shipping evidence passes.

## Leverage score
For every opportunity model:
- qualified buyer density
- offer/channel fit
- organic discovery potential
- repeatability
- gross margin after platform/payment/fulfillment costs
- activation friction
- ongoing support burden
- platform concentration risk
- rights/compliance burden
- measurement quality

No single synthetic score may override a hard stop.

## State machine
DISCOVERED -> OFFICIAL_TERMS_VERIFIED -> PRODUCT_FIT_VERIFIED -> ECONOMICS_MODELED -> LISTING_PREPARED -> OWNER_GATE_IF_REQUIRED -> PUBLISHED -> TRAFFIC_OBSERVED -> PAID -> FULFILLED -> NET_REALIZED

## Deep-sea rules
- Opportunity is not access.
- Account creation is not publication.
- Publication is not traffic.
- Traffic is not a customer.
- Gross sales are not realized profit.
- Never fabricate reviews, installs, users, demand, scarcity or partner relationships.
- Never spam communities or cold-contact at scale.
- Reverify current platform terms before activation.
- Prefer diversified channels over dependence on one gatekeeper.
- Preserve a canonical owned storefront/customer path whenever practical.

## Armada behavior
CARTOGRAPHER scouts.
INSPECTOR verifies fit/rules.
PURSER models economics.
TRANSLATOR prepares listings.
Owner crosses gates requiring identity, attestations, contracts, payout/tax setup or spend.
SIGNAL measures.
ADMIRAL scales evidenced winners and diversifies concentration risk.
