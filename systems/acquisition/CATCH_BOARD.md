# MINT Catch Board

## Objective
Turn qualified external attention into verified fulfilled customers with zero acquisition spend until evidence justifies a different mandate.

## Funnel
VISITED -> CHECKOUT_STARTED -> PAID -> FULFILLED -> PROVIDER_AVAILABLE -> SETTLED

These states are not interchangeable.

## Line scorecard
For each source:
- external visits observed
- checkout starts
- verified paid orders
- fulfilled orders
- provider-available amount
- settled amount
- refunds
- realized economics

## Allocation rule
Do not reward a line merely for existing or being indexed.
Prefer, in order:
1. verified paid + fulfilled
2. checkout starts from genuine external traffic
3. genuine external visits
4. impressions/discovery evidence

## Experiment discipline
- one clear intent per line
- standalone usefulness
- distinct source tag
- no fake scarcity/social proof
- no keyword-stuffed doorway pages
- no paid traffic without authorization
- retire or rewrite repeated zero-signal lines after enough observation
- expand winning customer problems/angles, not duplicate pages

## First catch
The first milestone remains one genuine non-owner external human progressing through the funnel. A bot, owner visit, generated checkout, or test event is not a catch.
