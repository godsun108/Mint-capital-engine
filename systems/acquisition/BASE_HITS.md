# BASE HITS — MINT Small-Pond Distribution Doctrine

## Principle
Base hits score runs.

MINT should not require a viral launch, giant marketplace win, or single dominant channel to survive. Build a diversified stream of small, measurable, lawful wins.

## Mission
Continuously discover and test small distribution opportunities whose:
- direct acquisition spend is $0 under the current mandate,
- audience/product fit is plausible and evidenceable,
- publishing method is permitted,
- downside is bounded,
- measurement is possible.

## Small ponds
- long-tail search intents
- genuinely useful free tools and field notes
- relevant product/resource directories
- developer catalogs and showcases
- niche marketplaces
- launch directories
- template/asset ecosystems
- permitted community resource threads
- complementary-product bundles
- disclosed affiliate/referral ecosystems
- newsletters/curators/creators where outreach is authorized
- open-source discovery surfaces where the contribution itself is legitimate and useful
- product integrations and ecosystem directories
- local/niche physical channels when physical offers become eligible

## Base-hit loop
SCOUT -> RULES VERIFY -> FIT CHECK -> PREPARE VALUE -> PUBLISH IF AUTHORIZED -> ATTRIBUTE -> OBSERVE -> KEEP / IMPROVE / RETIRE

## Scoreboard
A base hit must advance a real runner:
1. genuine qualified external visit
2. checkout start
3. verified payment
4. successful fulfillment
5. repeat/cross-sell/referral
6. realized net economic value

Page count, submissions, impressions, bots, owner activity and unverified checkouts are not runs.

## Portfolio discipline
- many low-cost experiments
- small reversible bets
- diversify channel dependence
- preserve successful evergreen lines
- retire repeated zero-signal lines
- replicate the customer problem/angle behind a winner, not duplicate thin pages
- Deep Sea and Base Hits run concurrently

## Hard stops
No spam.
No unsolicited mass outreach.
No fake reviews, users, downloads, customers, scarcity or engagement.
No rule circumvention.
No account/identity/contract attestations without owner action.
No spend without applicable authorization.
No thin doorway-page farms.
No promotional posting into communities that prohibit it.

## Authorization interpretation
The owner's full-force acquisition authorization permits autonomous zero-spend research, building, packaging, owned publishing, rule verification, measurement and preparation across lawful channels.

It does not override financial, identity, contractual, platform, supplier, tax, payout, regulated-product, spam, or owner-attestation gates.
