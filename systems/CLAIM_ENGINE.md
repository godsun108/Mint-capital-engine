# CLAIM ENGINE v0.1

Purpose: locate and pursue money that may already be legitimately claimable.

Adapters are grouped by authoritative source class:
- official unclaimed-property programs
- legitimate refunds/overpayments
- rebates/incentives
- reimbursements
- tax-credit/refund research
- receivables supported by genuine invoices/contracts/work

The engine must not assert entitlement from a search hit alone. Each hit is a CANDIDATE until identity, ownership, eligibility, documentation, deadline and claim procedure are verified.

Automation target:
SEARCH → MATCH → VERIFY → DOCUMENT CHECKLIST → DRAFT/PREFILL → HUMAN ATTESTATION → SUBMIT → FOLLOW UP → SETTLE

No fabricated claims, identities, invoices, expenses, withholding, dependents, ownership, eligibility or tax facts.
