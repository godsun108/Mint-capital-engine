# MINT Capital Armada

The Armada is a portfolio of specialized economic missions sharing one truth system, one permission model, and one allocator.

## Command doctrine
**Pig, not hog. Evidence before scale. Preserve the fleet.**

Every vessel must create or pursue at least one measurable form of economic value: cashflow, reusable assets, productive resources, verified savings, or decision-quality evidence.

No vessel may convert projections into claimed capital. No vessel may expand its permissions merely because an opportunity is attractive.

## Fleet
- **Foundry** — owned digital-product cashflow.
- **Lighthouse** — free utilities and intent content that earn attention.
- **Dockyard** — small software/API/micro-SaaS discovery and production.
- **Workboat** — productized services with verifiable fulfillment.
- **Licensor** — reusable assets and licensing opportunities.
- **Referral** — permitted, disclosed affiliate/referral opportunities.
- **Quartermaster** — grants, credits, programs, infrastructure and resource access.
- **Salvage** — cost reduction and substitution.
- **Treasury** — compounding research; execution remains approval-gated.

## Armada loop
SCOUT → VERIFY → TEST → BUILD/PREPARE → DEPLOY WHEN AUTHORIZED → OBSERVE → ACCOUNT → SCALE/RETIRE → REDEPLOY ATTENTION

## Fleet accounting
Keep projected value, awards, revenue, costs, provider-available funds, settled cash, received credits/resources, verified savings, and reusable assets separate.

The Armada wins by surviving long enough to discover repeatable edges.
