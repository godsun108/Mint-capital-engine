# Brand OS — MINT Multi-Brand System

## Principle
MINT is the economic and production engine. It does not need to be the customer-facing name of every product.

Brand architecture follows customer relevance, category clarity and durable differentiation—not a desire to manufacture the appearance of unrelated companies.

## Brand modes

### MASTER
Customer-facing MINT.
Use when the engine's existing identity improves clarity/trust.

Example:
MINT Shop

### ENDORSED
A category/product brand with a quiet MINT endorsement.

Pattern:
[Brand] — by MINT
or
[Brand], a MINT product

Use when a distinct category identity helps while shared provenance remains useful.

### STANDALONE
A product-relevant brand can lead without MINT in the primary name.

Use when:
- the product has a distinct audience/problem,
- the name communicates the outcome/category better,
- a different visual/verbal system materially improves fit.

Standalone does not mean fake independence. Legal seller/provenance, required disclosures and transaction identity remain accurate.

## Naming gate
Before adopting a customer-facing name:
1. define audience/problem/category;
2. generate candidates;
3. screen for obvious confusion and undesirable meaning;
4. search current web/product usage;
5. check relevant domain/handle availability when useful;
6. perform appropriate trademark screening before consequential commercial investment;
7. record why the name was selected.

A repository name is not trademark clearance.

## Brand record
Every brand/product identity should define:
- brand_id
- mode: MASTER / ENDORSED / STANDALONE
- public_name
- descriptor
- audience
- promise
- personality
- verbal rules
- visual tokens
- parent/provenance relationship
- products
- domains/surfaces
- naming/trademark status
- evidence/status

## Shared trust layer
Regardless of public brand:
- truthful seller identity where required
- accurate product claims
- accurate pricing
- no fabricated customers/reviews/traction
- consistent support and fulfillment facts
- privacy/accessibility/security practices appropriate to the surface
- source attribution and economics flow into the same MINT ledger

## Design tokens
Brand OS separates:
1. structural tokens — accessibility, spacing, responsive behavior, component semantics;
2. master tokens — optional MINT visual language;
3. brand tokens — palette/type/shape/imagery/voice for a specific identity;
4. product tokens — tightly scoped campaign/product variations.

Agents should inherit structure while allowing brand-level creative variation.

## Anti-patterns
- slapping MINT on every product regardless of fit;
- inventing dozens of brands before products have differentiated audiences;
- pretending multiple brands are independent sellers to create fake competition;
- fake founders/personas/testimonials;
- cloning competitor trade dress;
- naming without basic collision screening;
- changing brand merely because a product has not yet found demand.

## Objective
One production engine.
Many truthful customer-relevant identities.
Shared economics, evidence, fulfillment and governance.
