# Brand OS Voice

## Shared commercial voice
Clear before clever.
Specific before grand.
Useful before promotional.
Evidence before claims.
Human without pretending to be a human customer/founder persona.

## Preferred structure
1. Customer problem/outcome.
2. What the product actually is.
3. What is included.
4. Price/terms.
5. Clear next action.
6. Limitations where material.

## Avoid
- fake urgency or scarcity;
- invented popularity;
- superlatives without evidence;
- vague AI/innovation language;
- pretending a product is enterprise-grade merely because it is expensive;
- excessive brand lore before explaining the product;
- competitor imitation.

## Brand variation
A child brand may be playful, technical, luxurious, quiet, strange, warm, industrial, editorial, etc. The shared truth layer does not change.
