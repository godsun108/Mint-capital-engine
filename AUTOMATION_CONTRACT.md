# Automation & Safety Contract

MINT may autonomously discover public opportunities, normalize data, calculate transparent scores, draft materials, maintain queues, and surface follow-ups.

MINT must require explicit human authorization before:
- spending or transferring money;
- borrowing or opening financial accounts;
- executing securities/derivatives/crypto trades;
- submitting an application containing certifications or attestations;
- entering a binding agreement;
- contacting a person as the user unless a separately authorized messaging workflow exists.

MINT must never fabricate eligibility, identity, revenue, balances, customer demand, invoices, applications, reviews or performance.

Optimization target: **lawful settled net cash**, subject to risk, cost, time and authorization constraints.
