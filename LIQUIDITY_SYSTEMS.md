# LIQUIDITY SYSTEMS — Portfolio Contract

MINT should run multiple independent, lawful paths to **settled USD**, because no single source is reliable.

## CREATE
1. **PRODUCT** — create/sell/fulfill digital products and software.
2. **SERVICE** — package services, qualify leads, prepare offers and fulfillment.
3. **COMMISSION** — track commissionable work from lead through paid commission.
4. **LICENSE** — package licensable software/content/IP and track royalties.
5. **MARKETPLACE** — identify lawful resale opportunities after fees, costs and fulfillment.
6. **SUBSCRIPTION** — recurring products/services with churn and net-cash tracking.

## CLAIM
7. **RECEIVABLE** — invoice, follow up and reconcile amounts legitimately owed.
8. **REFUND** — identify legitimate refundable charges/deposits/overpayments and prepare claims.
9. **REBATE** — discover rebates/incentives, verify eligibility and prepare redemption.
10. **UNCLAIMED** — search official unclaimed-property channels and prepare legitimate claims.
11. **GRANT** — discover grants, verify eligibility, assemble evidence and draft applications.
12. **REIMBURSE** — track legitimate reimbursable expenses/benefits and supporting evidence.

## ACCESS
13. **FINANCING** — compare legitimate financing offers on net proceeds, APR/fees, collateral, repayment and covenants.
14. **RECEIVABLE FINANCE** — evaluate financing against genuine receivables/contracts where available.
15. **ASSET LIQUIDITY** — evaluate sale/lease/financing paths for assets the user actually controls.

Borrowed cash is tagged **LIABILITY-BACKED LIQUIDITY** and never counted as earned income.

## REDUCE CASH LEAKAGE
16. **RENEWAL** — identify recurring charges/renewals for review.
17. **NEGOTIATE** — surface bills/contracts where a legitimate renegotiation could reduce future outflow.
18. **CASH TIMING** — prioritize collections and obligations by timing without misrepresenting solvency.

Savings are tracked separately from cash inflows.

## Execution contract
Every system uses:
DISCOVER → VERIFY → QUALIFY → ECONOMICS → PREPARE → APPROVE IF REQUIRED → EXECUTE → FOLLOW UP → SETTLE → RECONCILE

Every candidate records:
- source/provenance
- gross potential
- all known costs/fees/taxes where estimable
- probability estimate and basis
- human minutes
- days-to-cash
- upfront cash
- restrictions/obligations
- execution state
- settlement evidence

No system may fabricate eligibility, revenue, invoices, receivables, customers, identity, collateral, ownership, certifications, reviews, or balances.
