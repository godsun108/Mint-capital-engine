# Intentional apparel: personal feng shui and signature house philosophy

Status: CREATIVE DESIGN PRINCIPLE, not a verified health or energy-effect claim.

## Three distinct briefs
1. **Personal wardrobe:** owner-specific feng shui interpretation, preferred colors, materials, silhouettes, symbols and practical context. Do not infer a traditional feng shui chart or favorable elements from unconfirmed data. Ask for the specific tradition/method and preferences before assigning auspicious colors or directions.
2. **Signature house:** original meaningful visual language, balanced compositions, conscious sourcing, harmony, symbolic embroidery and optional customer storytelling. Offer choice, not a one-size-fits-all prescription.
3. **Occasion commerce:** seasonal, gift and trend-oriented items are not required to use feng shui principles; avoid attaching spiritual claims to generic commercial products.

## Apparel pilot application
- For owner hoodie and matching sweatpants: create a personalized color/material/symbol placement brief before locking the final garment artwork.
- Explore embroidered Flamekeeper motifs, discreet inner cuff/hem glyphs, tonal repeating patterns and comfortable natural-fiber fabrics, subject to supplier verification.
- Separate personally meaningful symbols from universally marketed promises. Never claim garments attract wealth, cure illness, or change energy as proven facts.
- Preserve brand naming flexibility Aurel / Aurelion / TBD and protect the original Orbital Garden tote unchanged.
- Continue supplier/material qualification and first-sale execution in parallel; personalization should not block commercial experiments.
