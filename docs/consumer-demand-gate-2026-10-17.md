# Consumer market validation gate — October 17, 2026

Status: research only. Keep consumer products out of Foundry/MINT customer-facing developer/business catalog.

## New competitive evidence
Etsy vacation-rental welcome-book-template category shows 5,000+ results, including digital offers around $1.99–$31.19, often heavily discounted. Source: https://www.etsy.com/market/vacation_rental_welcome_book_template (accessed 2026-10-17). This is evidence of competition and asking prices, NOT sales volume.

A newly listed interactive welcome book offers mobile and printable PDF, Canva editing, interactive TOC, click-to-call and Google Maps links for $14.99. Source: https://www.etsy.com/listing/4510590805/welcome-book-template-editable (accessed 2026-10-17). Thus interactive navigation and operational information alone are not differentiators.

Marketplace Insights reportedly exposes 30-day searches and listing counts with 15 free keyword lookups/week for eligible Etsy sellers. Seller help: https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/35122361353239-How-Do-I-Use-Etsy-s-Marketplace-Insights-Tool

## Decision
Do not build or list a generic welcome book, journal or coloring book. First collect measurable narrow-keyword demand, dated reviews, competitive feature gaps, platform fees and a distribution path. Candidate narrower business problems to investigate: guest self-check-in troubleshooting, property-specific operating procedures, multilingual guest instructions. These are hypotheses, not validated demand.

No storefront changes, deployment, expenditure, product or revenue claims. Existing MINT first-dollar checkout and fulfillment verification remain higher priority.
