## Research-first funnel
1. Identify buyer intent in a narrowly defined consumer category with dated sources, competitor listings, observed price ranges, reviews and realistic margins.
2. Distinguish searches/listings from confirmed purchases. No claims of demand without evidence.
3. Check originality, resale rights, AI disclosure requirements, permitted distribution and fees.
4. Score differentiation, purchase urgency, competition, repeat-purchase potential, organic acquisition access and zero-spend fit.
5. Only then prototype one consumer product, approve separate customer-facing identity/channel, and test secure delivery.
6. Track actual paid, fulfilled and settled revenue separately from traffic.
