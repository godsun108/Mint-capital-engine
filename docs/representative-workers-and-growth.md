# Representative Commerce Workers and Growth Programs

## Worker lifecycle
PROPOSED → OWNER_APPROVED → CREDENTIAL_SCOPED → ACTIVE → SUSPENDED/REVOKED.
Every worker has an entity represented, supervisor, role, allowed actions, prohibited actions and immutable action evidence. A public-facing AI must identify itself accurately and must not claim legal authority it does not possess.

## Store operating model
Each store has a Store Manager, Catalog Representative, Distribution Representative, Customer Concierge, Affiliate Manager and Revenue Controller. One worker may initially fill multiple compatible roles, but payment/reconciliation review should remain independently checked. Stores are channel/brand presentations of canonical catalog offers, not separate payment ledgers.

## Affiliate pilot specification (not live)
- Invite a small number of approved affiliates to share a truthful offer with clear advertising disclosures.
- Give each affiliate a non-sensitive referral identifier and channel-specific link.
- Attribute conversions only after provider-confirmed successful payments; define a clear attribution window and handling of competing referrals before launch.
- Track refunds, chargebacks, suspicious self-referrals and commission liabilities.
- Keep commission payable distinct from paid and settled cash. Human approves rates, terms and payouts.
- Verify applicable platform rules, advertising disclosures and tax reporting before accepting affiliates.

## Subscription pilot specification (not live)
- Do not turn one-off downloads into recurring charges without ongoing value.
- Candidate ongoing value: a regularly maintained creator/business toolkit with substantive releases and support, only once deliverable capacity is established.
- Show price, billing frequency, renewal/cancellation and refund terms clearly; obtain affirmative recurring-payment consent.
- Use provider-hosted customer management and verified billing events; grant/revoke entitlement according to policy.
- Maintain one-time product availability for customers who do not want subscriptions.

## First practical sequence
1. Continue direct-storefront sales and Gumroad seller-account onboarding with owner authentication.
2. Add account records for legal seller identity and customer-facing brand, verified against actual documents.
3. Implement affiliate attribution in a test-only environment before public invitations or promised commissions.
4. Pilot a subscription only after demonstrating recurring customer value and completing provider sandbox QA.
