## Assessment
1. Vacation-rental templates: strongest **specific business problem**, but highly crowded and feature-rich incumbent bundles. Proceed only with interview/keyword proof of unmet need, not a generic template.
2. Travel journals: buyer-facing market exists but low prices and high competition. Test niche-specific intent; don't build yet.
3. Coloring: lowest priority absent a defensible niche, original art and distribution.
