# MINT Digital Goods Foundry — Product Factory v0.1

Goal: a repeatable, evidence-first pipeline for useful Notion templates and digital downloads, not a mass-upload content farm. This specification authorizes design and local prototype work only, not publishing, paid API use, external messaging or live checkout changes.

## Product formats
- Notion-native page/database templates with import instructions and linked CSV/Markdown fallback; validate actual Notion behavior before claiming a one-click duplicate link.
- PDF guides, checklists and workbooks (accessible text, links, page breaks).
- Spreadsheets with tested formulas, sample data and clear assumptions.
- Markdown/CSV/JSON starter kits, SOPs and planners.
- Bundles with versioned manifest, license, support/contact and changelog.

## Production line
1. **Demand Scout**: capture a specific audience, problem, competing/free alternatives, search intent, evidence and differentiation. No invented demand or sales claims.
2. **Offer Architect**: promise, outcome, scope, price hypothesis, effort estimate, fulfillment type and support burden.
3. **Product Designer**: schema, information architecture, examples, accessibility and format-specific acceptance tests.
4. **Maker**: generate original assets; track source material and license. No copyrighted template cloning.
5. **QA Critic**: test Notion import/database relationships, spreadsheet formulas, PDF links and file integrity. Independent review; reject generic filler.
6. **Packager**: produce deliverables, previews, README, license, changelog, product metadata and SHA-256 manifest.
7. **Commerce Adapter**: prepare catalog proposal and sandbox fulfillment; require explicit owner approval before activating a live offer.
8. **Distribution Agent**: draft platform-appropriate copy and useful free samples; no unsolicited bulk messages, fake reviews or unapproved posts.
9. **Revenue Analyst**: attribute traffic and reconcile live provider-confirmed transactions, fees, refunds and delivered receipts; unknown != zero.
10. **Iteration Agent**: use observed conversion, refunds, customer feedback and support load to improve or retire products.

## First three candidate prototypes (not yet market validated)
A. Notion Personal Command Center — goals → projects → weekly plan → daily actions; clean mobile use, examples and CSV fallback.
B. Small Business Launch Kit — checklists, budget sheet, launch tracker and customer journey map; complements existing $5 site checklist.
C. Creator Release Tracker — asset inventory, content calendar, release checklist and simple revenue log.

## Gates and owner control
IDEA → RESEARCHED → PROTOTYPE → QA_PASS → SAMPLE_REVIEW → OWNER_APPROVED → TEST_CHECKOUT → LIVE → VERIFIED_SALES. Each gate stores evidence, responsible reviewer, date and unresolved exceptions. Agents can reject a product. Only approved products enter the live catalog.

## Economics
For each product estimate build hours, per-sale platform/payment fees, support/refund costs, distribution cost, and net contribution; mark estimates separately from observed results. Avoid indiscriminate SKU proliferation: begin with one flagship and two supporting goods.

## Notion-specific
Export interoperable Markdown and CSV; test property types, relations, rollups, views and formulas inside Notion with authorized access. Without Notion API authorization, produce an importable kit and explicit setup guide; do not claim the workspace/template was created in the user's Notion account.
