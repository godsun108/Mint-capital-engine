# Commerce Management Division

This is a role and account control-plane specification, **not seven running autonomous agents or five authenticated accounts**. Reuse MINT's existing market, merchant, product and auditor lifecycle. No new payment ledger.

## Operational sequence
1. Commerce Director chooses an existing ACTIVE mandate-approved offer.
2. Marketplace Manager creates truthful listings for each eligible channel, subject to platform rules.
3. Social Distribution Manager drafts educational content with tagged product-specific links; owner approves external publishing.
4. Conversion Manager reviews real visit → checkout → provider-confirmed payment → fulfillment evidence.
5. Customer Concierge handles inbound support drafts and routes disputes.
6. Product Manager uses feedback to propose tested product improvements, without silently replacing a paid deliverable.
7. Revenue Controller deduplicates provider transaction IDs, reconciles fees/refunds and reports settled vs paid separately.

## Owner account onboarding checklist
- Select the legal seller identity and public brand. Do not represent a brand hypothesis as a registered company.
- Owner opens/authenticates seller account and accepts terms; complete tax, payout and identity verification privately.
- Confirm permitted digital-file formats, marketplace fees, refund rules and disclosure.
- Approve first listing, public post and spending cap independently.
- Store tokens only in appropriate secret manager; give least-privilege scopes and maintain revocation.
- Start with zero paid advertising; track each channel separately.

## First rollout
Existing: MINT direct checkout, owned Pages. Prepared but not connected: Gumroad, Etsy. Candidate: Instagram or other owner-selected social channel. Start with one approved external seller account, validate fulfillment and support, then replicate. No invented account creation, publications or purchases.
