## Acceptance gates
- Research demand using observable buyer searches, competing offers, reviews, pricing, and permitted marketplace data. Record dated source URLs; no invented sales counts.
- Build and inspect every actual deliverable, including page dimensions, printable margins, accessibility and a real download test.
- Distinct design and rights check: no copied templates, trademarked characters, scraped art or unsupported resale rights.
- Human approval of price, copy, licensing, refunds, platform fees, seller eligibility and marketplace listing before publication.
- Verify test-mode checkout, webhook, access control, file delivery, refund/failure and attribution before live sale.
- Track VISITED, CHECKOUT_STARTED, PAID, FULFILLED, SETTLED separately. No claimed income until independent payment evidence.
- Compare margin and conversion against existing $5–$19 MINT offers before scaling.

## Distribution experiment
Phase 1: Owned website and contextual, helpful content on authorized channels. Phase 2: evaluate Etsy marketplace discovery and all fees; do not open an account or incur listing costs without approval. Gumroad/Printful are fulfillment or selling infrastructure, not evidence of demand.

## Product family architecture
One product spec -> reusable PDF layout + optional CSV -> ZIP artifact -> catalog metadata -> preview image -> listing copy -> fulfillment check -> measured sales. Avoid mass-producing near-duplicate spam.
