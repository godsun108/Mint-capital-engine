# AEON / 2026 — Fire Horse Artwork Specification

Status: ORIGINAL ART DIRECTION / NO PRODUCTION ARTWORK OR SPEND.

## Core idea
2026 is the Bing Wu (丙午) Fire Horse year (lunar new year begins 2026-02-17). The owner's December 2026 birthday falls within it. Use Fire Horse as artistic context, not as a claim that the wearer was born in a Fire Horse year. Owner's birth year is Water Rooster.

## Design layers
- Hero: two **computed** skies, Origin 1993 Las Vegas and Becoming 2026 Port St. Lucie. Planetary coordinates and preliminary horizon projections now live in connect-demo/aeon-2026-planetary-sky.json and SVG previews. THESE ARE NOT YET COMPLETE STAR MAPS.
- Fire Horse: an original galloping horse drawn in one flowing, continuous silhouette. Mane transitions into copper flames; body uses minimal geometrical rhythm informed by Flamekeeper design language. Avoid generic clipart and crowded graphics.
- Ember: warm gold/copper embroidery, tonal ember red accent; Fire/Earth are personalized design inspiration, not objective luck guarantees.
- Heritage: 33 micro-stars (counted on final art map), birth-to-birthday inscription, discreet 丙午 inscription.

## Placement hierarchy
- Hoodie back: Origin sky dominates; Fire Horse placed below or integrated with the outer circular border, not over astronomical data.
- Hoodie chest: simplified Fire Horse/Flamekeeper crest suitable for small embroidery.
- Sweatpants outer leg: Becoming sky, with a flame trail motif and discreet date text.
- Inner label: location, local date/time, astronomical-data attribution and garment care; avoid inaccurate claims about sky visibility in afternoon.
