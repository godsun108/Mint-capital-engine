# Brand-flexible launch decision — 2026-10-10

## Decision
Keep **Aurel / Aurelion / TBD** as un-cleared working naming candidates. Do not register, print, publish or spend on a public name without screening and owner approval. The visual system can be portable across names.

## Product-first priority
1. Design a signature premium hoodie with original recovered pattern/embroidery, with a matching sweatpant concept. User should want to wear these personally; do not assume they are ready to manufacture.
2. Parallel fast-launch test: qualify one original graphic tee on a real supplier variant and verify checkout, shipping and margin before publishing.
3. Add other lines/brands (sport, bohemian, music, tea, creative merchandise) through MINT's shared brand/product routing, not duplicate commerce backends.

## Acceptance tests
- A named supplier/variant with fiber composition, costs and lead time independently verified.
- Rights-tracked source artwork, production files and embroidery feasibility verified against provider specs.
- Matching outfit fit/quality and return policy evidence; samples only after spend approval.
- Accurate product page, payment, tax/shipping disclosures, order and fulfillment verification.
- First actual paid customer order and fulfilled delivery recorded before revenue claims.

## Constraints
Zero marketing spend until unit economics and fulfillment are proven. Preserve Orbital Garden original tote unchanged. EarthChain token commerce remains an optional gated integration; no unsupported live token-redemption claim. Avoid unnecessary new brand repositories.
