- A specific interactive Canva welcome guide is listed for $14.99 with mobile PDF, tap navigation, clickable phone/maps. Its page reports two listing reviews dated July 19 and September 7 2026; the seller reports 36 shop sales, not sales of this product. https://www.etsy.com/listing/4510590805/welcome-book-template-editable
- A multilingual five-language interactive welcome book is already listed for $12.30 sale price; five languages and mobile guide are NOT defensible unique differentiators. https://www.etsy.com/listing/4591490339/airbnb-welcome-book-template-canva-5
- An extensive 60-page host bundle includes guest messages, rules, checklists, signs and business workbook. Even operational bundles are not empty market gaps. https://www.etsy.com/listing/4575355478/editable-vacation-rental-welcome-book
- Etsy Marketplace Insights officially offers 30-day search and listing counts, 15 free searches weekly for eligible sellers on mobile web. https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/35122361353239-How-Do-I-Use-Etsy-s-Marketplace-Insights-Tool
- Etsy reports experimental access for some sellers to search conversion rates and *purchased* listing price ranges, not universal access. https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/10603291042967-Newly-Crafted-Etsy-Updates-for-Your-Shop

## Decision
Generic welcome books, multilingual variants and operational host bundles all have live competition. Do not manufacture a supposed gap. Buyer-specific research needed before any consumer SKU. Favor interviewing 5-10 real hosts about repeated guest questions, turnaround bottlenecks and willingness to pay; compare against existing automation/property-management solutions.
