# Consumer Digital Goods — Evidence Pass 1 (2026-10-17)

**Research only.** MINT/Foundry developer and business storefront unchanged. No product publication, new brand, paid listing, affiliate enrollment or revenue claim.

## Evidence hierarchy
- Etsy search results/listing counts and observed prices = supply/competition, **not units sold**.
- Etsy trend report = change in search interest, **not proof of digital conversion**.
- Third-party SEO keyword estimates = directional only, not audited Etsy transactions.
- Reviews on individual listings = some purchase/customer engagement evidence, but neither monthly units nor revenue.
- Need Marketplace Insights (last 30 days Etsy searches and listings), accessible in Shop Manager on mobile web; 15 free keyword queries/week for eligible sellers: https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/35122361353239-How-Do-I-Use-Etsy-s-Marketplace-Insights-Tool

## Three-niche comparison
### 1. Vacation rental welcome books — PRIORITY: targeted differentiation research
- Etsy vacation book templates results: 5,000+ items across broader mixed category. Examples of editable Airbnb/VRBO guides $1.99, $4.41, $5.25, $6.19, $13.60, varying sales/review history; do not treat these as standardized averages.
- Source: https://www.etsy.com/market/vacation_book_templates (checked 2026-10-17).
- Verified individual example: 12-page editable PDF at ~$5.95 sale price, with one listing review: https://www.etsy.com/listing/1781906762/editable-vacation-rental-welcome-book
- Hypothesis: higher-value **operational** guest information bundle for specific hosts (accessibility, house systems, maintenance/turnover checklists, editable instructions), not just decorative welcome pages. Avoid unsupported safety/compliance guarantees.
- Gate: inspect search-to-listing ratio for narrower keywords, seller rights/Canva terms, and whether a buyer would pay enough after fees.

### 2. Memory/travel journals — PRIORITY: second
- Etsy's Mar 17 2026 spring/summer trend report: 'traveler journal' searches doubled; 'memory journals' +189%. These include physical journals; digital-specific demand unverified.
- Source: https://www.etsy.com/ca/seller-handbook/article/1473931456647
- Hypothesis: travel photo memory journal with actual fillable/printable layouts, not generic prompt sheets.
- Gate: inspect digital-only search results and recent reviews before prototype.

### 3. Specialty printable coloring — PRIORITY: third
- Third-party RankHero Aug 2026 estimate: ~301k monthly searches for broad 'coloring books', ~287k listings reported in intro, with inconsistent other listing counts elsewhere on page. Treat as directional and do not reuse precise competition number as verified fact.
- Source: https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/coloring-books
- Hypothesis: original niche illustration sets with recognizable artistic quality and lawful rights. Crowded low-ticket category; no mass generic output.
- Gate: verify narrowly targeted demand, rights and differentiation; no art generation yet.
