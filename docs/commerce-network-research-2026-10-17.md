2. Net commission or margin after fees, refunds, support and tax reserve.
3. Recurring duration, churn risk, attribution window and payout threshold.
4. No-upfront-cost fit and iPhone-friendly operations.
5. Terms, geographic eligibility, permissions, disclosures and refund exposure.
6. Real tracking and settlement evidence.
