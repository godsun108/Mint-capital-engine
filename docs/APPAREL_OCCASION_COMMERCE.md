# Multi-tier apparel and occasion commerce

Status: RESEARCH / PLANNING ONLY. No listing, forecast, supplier, or holiday sale verified.

## Two parallel portfolios
**House / signature:** Aurel or Aurelion (working names, uncleared). Original visual language, natural-material priorities, garment quality, premium design, long-lived core products.
**Occasion / commercial:** separate product lines or customer-facing brands for holidays, family titles, hobbies, gifting, memes and timely demand. No requirement that every product use signature brand identity.

## Dimensions of the catalog
- Expressions: masculine, feminine, shared; each requires fit/sizing validation, not merely recolors.
- Use cases: streetwear, athletic/sport, comfort/lounge, everyday basics, luxury/high fashion.
- Tiers: accessible, midrange, premium, luxury. Tiers are evidence-based quality/price positioning, not arbitrary markups.
- Audiences: mom, dad, parents, grandparents, friends, partners, athletes, hobby communities and gifting audiences.
- Occasions: Halloween, Thanksgiving, winter holidays, New Year's, Valentine's, graduation, Mother's Day, Father's Day, weddings, birthdays, evergreen gifting.
- Styles: typographic, playful, minimal, embroidered, illustrative, patterned, luxury.

## Reuse-first operating loop
1. Calendar/idea intake in Command Center: event date, audience, product concept, demand hypothesis, target launch date and ship-by cutoffs.
2. VHE/STUDIO generates original creative alternatives with rights checks; recovered Flamekeeper/Aurel patterns reserved for suitable signature pieces.
3. MINT/PRESS matches available supplier variants and validates fabric, sizing, art constraints and shipping windows.
4. INSPECTOR and PURSER check rights, claims, taxes/fees, realistic unit contribution, customer service and return exposure.
5. Owner approves publication and spend; MINT routes listing and fulfillment through existing connected channels.
6. SIGNAL measures real clicks, conversions, refunds and settled sales; retire weak products, iterate winners.

## Priority and safeguards
- Protect signature brand positioning; use separate occasion collection/brand for generic gift merch.
- Never generate trademarked slogans/characters or imply official affiliation; rights screening required.
- No invented urgency, delivery guarantee, organic certifications, sales numbers, supplier availability or profits.
- Seasonal publishing gate must verify fulfillment lead time and advertised delivery promise.
- First commercial sprint: one evergreen gift tee/cap and one timely seasonal design only after supplier and channel qualification; avoid speculative catalog floods.
- Preserve Orbital Garden tote unchanged and keep blockchain token payments behind verification gates.

## Launch success
A verified paid and fulfilled order, measured unit economics and repeatable evidence-backed product launches; not simply many drafts or green CI checks.
