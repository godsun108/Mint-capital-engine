# First Customer Sprint — Evidence-Gated Plan

Status: PLANNED (not launched). Date: 2026-10-12.

## Verified baseline
- Connected live Stripe account returned zero payment intents and zero balance transactions at audit time.
- Only listed Stripe product is a synthetic MINT Genesis integration test; never advertise it as a real offer.
- Metricool has Instagram Luna Vale, YouTube Mechanical Obsessions, and Bluesky Foundry Tools connections. Connection is not proof of reach, conversions, or revenue.

## First commercial experiment
Offer one genuinely useful, deliverable digital product or service. Candidate: a personalized recipe adaptation pack based on Alchemy Kitchen, subject to verifying capability, licensing, pricing and fulfillment. This is a hypothesis, not an approved public launch.

## Gates before publishing
1. Verify exact product deliverables and manual/automatic fulfillment, with human review where necessary.
2. Validate customer demand via non-spam outreach and feedback; record evidence.
3. Calculate fees, labor, refund policy, support burden and contribution margin.
4. Create distinct production Stripe product/price (do not repurpose synthetic test product).
5. Run a clearly labeled test-mode checkout and end-to-end delivery/refund tests; never create a real charge just for testing.
6. Obtain owner approval for public price, customer-facing claims and launch.
7. Publish a clear product page with accurate examples, privacy terms, support and disclosures.
8. Track source-tagged visits, checkouts, successful settled payments, refunds, fees and net receipts.
