# Aurel Essentials — controlled first apparel pilot

Status: DESIGN BRIEF / RESEARCH, NOT LISTED OR FOR SALE
Owner approval required for publication, payments, supplier orders, token checkout, or spend.

## Mission
Develop the user's first Aurel outfit: a genuinely desirable hoodie and matching sweatpants, with verified natural-fiber sourcing, original art, and truthful fulfillment. In parallel, qualify three fast-to-test graphic tees. Preserve the original Orbital Garden tote and all existing files without modification.

## Brand and product intent
- Aurel: everyday premium comfort; original minimal and expressive graphics; future Bohemian garments.
- Initial SKUs (candidates only): AUREL-HOODIE-001; AUREL-SWEATPANT-001; AUREL-TEE-RANCH-001; AUREL-TEE-GOOD-GUY-001; AUREL-TEE-NOT-FAMOUS-001.
- Graphic slogans: "I LOVE RANCH"; "GOOD GUY. MOMS LOVE ME."; "I'M NOT FAMOUS ANYMORE".
- Organic cotton, hemp, linen and other natural fibers are preferred. Record fiber percentages, weight, dyes/finishes, origin, certifications with source evidence, supplier and variant. Do not label a product organic or sustainable without substantiation.
- Hoodie/pants must be wearable as a matching set; evaluate fit, color, weight, sizing, care, wash shrinkage and seam/print/embroidery durability.
- No unsupported hemp or natural-fiber claims, and no assumption a matching supplier set exists.
- Confirm Aurel name clearance before public brand launch.

## Qualification gates
- G0: original creative brief + reference provenance + owner design approval
- G1: live supplier variant, material composition and certification evidence
- G2: exact print/embroidery template, artwork dimensions, placement and QA
- G3: product and shipping quote, tax/fees assumptions, contribution and return policy
- G4: accurate mockups, checkout and fulfillment simulation, owner approval
- G5: controlled publish and first verified paid-to-delivered order, then measured promotion
