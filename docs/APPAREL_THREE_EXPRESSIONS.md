# Apparel creative universe: three expressions

Status: WORKING DESIGN FRAMEWORK; no brand names or product listings approved.

## FORCE — hyper masculine expression
Structured outerwear, heavyweight hoodies, tailored and wide-leg trousers, bold pattern scale, dense tonal embroidery, strong shoulders. Explore fit and size ranges on actual bodies, not gender stereotypes.

## GRACE — hyper feminine expression
Sculptural dresses, flowing robes, fitted and relaxed separates, soft draping, expressive embroidery, layered textile art, and practical comfort. Explore actual fit, support, transparency and sizing.

## UNITY — shared expression
Natural-fiber everyday sets, oversized tops, relaxed trousers, scarves, embroidered shirts and adaptable garments. Avoid assuming one fit block suits everyone; grade and test silhouettes independently.
