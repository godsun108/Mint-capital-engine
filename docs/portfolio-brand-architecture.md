# Portfolio Brand Architecture — v0.1

**MINT is the operating engine, not a mandatory consumer-facing brand.**

## Separation of concerns
- Engine: MINT handles research, candidate gating, artifact QA, catalog, attribution, fulfillment and provider-specific reconciliation.
- Brand: each offer may carry a distinct customer-facing brand, visual identity, positioning, support identity and domain when approved.
- Product: canonical offer ID and version remain stable internally; do not fork inventory or fabricate independent sales for rebranded listings.
- Channel: storefront, marketplace and campaign presentation are channel-specific. Preserve platform checkout and fulfillment rules.
- Evidence: normalize sales by provider transaction ID, offer ID, brand ID and channel ID; record fees, refunds and actual net proceeds. Never double count cross-listed orders.

## Candidate portfolio (working names, not trademark-cleared or launched)
- **MINT** — optional visible brand for technical/business tooling.
- **Launchlight** — small website and API launch checklists.
- **Daymark Studio** — productivity templates, planners and creator systems.
- **Foundry Express** — developer starters and software kits.
- **Groundwork Goods** — practical guides and bundles.

No brand is deemed cleared for use until domain, marketplace name and trademark checks. Do not claim independent companies or registered marks.

## Commercial release
1. Select a brand hypothesis based on customer language and channel fit.
2. Verify naming rights and marketplace rules.
3. Build a truthful branded preview from the canonical artifact.
4. Run independent QA and owner review.
5. Publish only through approved accounts and mandates.
6. Attribute customer feedback and purchases to the canonical offer while retaining brand/channel provenance.

**Do not** create a separate factory, duplicate payment ledger, or change live checkout names without reviewing the customer-facing disclosure and delivery experience.
