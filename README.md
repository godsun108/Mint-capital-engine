# MINT — Capital Engine

**Objective:** increase lawful, settled, spendable capital by turning real opportunities into measurable execution.

> FIND → VERIFY → SCORE → ACT → COLLECT → SETTLE → REINVEST

MINT is not a money printer and does not promise profit. It is an operating system for finding and executing legitimate revenue, funding, receivable, savings and capital opportunities while keeping expected money separate from cash actually available.

## v0.1

- opportunity pipeline
- deterministic opportunity scoring
- explicit status transitions
- expected value vs settled cash separation
- cost and human-time inputs
- local-first browser persistence
- cash-event ledger
- guardrails for financial actions

### Score

The initial score is a transparent heuristic based on probability of collection, net expected cash, time-to-cash, human hours and upfront cash required. It is an attention score, not a guarantee.

### Automation boundary

Discovery, normalization, ranking, drafting and reminders may become highly automated. Purchases, borrowing, applications/certifications, binding contracts, trades, transfers and other consequential financial actions require explicit human authorization.

## Planned engines

1. digital/software products and automated fulfillment
2. service/lead pipelines
3. receivables and collections
4. grants/rebates/public opportunities
5. lawful resale/arbitrage discovery
6. treasury/cash allocation
7. bounded investment analysis

MINT records settled capital only after the user marks a cash event settled.


## v0.2 — SCOUT
SCOUT v0.1 adds automated public-opportunity discovery. The first adapter ingests Grants.gov candidates on a six-hour schedule and presents them for human review before entering MINT's opportunity queue. Imported candidates start with zero assumed value/probability; discovery is not eligibility or expected cash.
